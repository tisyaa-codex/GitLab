

\# Code of Conduct



\## Our Pledge



We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone.



\## Our Standards



Examples of positive behaviour include:



\- Being respectful and friendly.

\- Accepting constructive feedback.

\- Helping other contributors.

\- Respecting different opinions.

\- Communicating professionally.



Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, offensive language, and disrespectful comments.



\## Reporting Issues



If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact:



\*\*Email:\*\* tisyamgopal@gmail.com



All reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially.



\## Responsibilities



All project contributors are expected to follow this Code of Conduct and maintain a positive environment.



\## Scope



This Code of Conduct applies to all project activities, discussions, and contributions.
